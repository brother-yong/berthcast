How it works

Start with the files you already export.

  1. Upload your files

    Use your inventory, sales, purchase-order and supplier exports in Excel or CSV. berthcast detects the columns automatically.

  2. Confirm the item matches

    Confirm suggested matches for items named differently across your files, and check the supplier lead times the report will use.

  3. Review and approve your order

    Check the suggested quantities against what you know, change any of them, then approve and print the order grouped by supplier.
