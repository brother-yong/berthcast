The problem

Where the money leaks out.

A stockout costs the sale, and often the customer with it — the line was ordered a week too late.

Overstock does the opposite: cash sits in the warehouse, and on chilled and frozen lines it walks out as waste.

Both happen for the same reason. The reorder logic lives in one person's head and one spreadsheet, and neither scales with the catalogue.

How it works

Three steps, from your files to a decision.

01

Upload your files

Inventory, sales and purchase orders, exactly as they come out of your system. No column mapping, no template to fill in.

02

An AI pipeline reads them

Messy sheets are normalised, then every line is scored on stock health against its demand and its supplier's real lead time.

03

Get the report and the order

A stock-health report plus exact purchase recommendations — quantities you can review, adjust, approve and print.
